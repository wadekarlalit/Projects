Bharati Vidyapeeth University Online MBA – UGC-Entitled Program

The Bharati Vidyapeeth Online MBA 2.0 is a two-year, UGC-DEB and AICTE-recognised programme created for professionals who want to strengthen their managerial and strategic capabilities. Delivered through an advanced digital learning platform and taught by experienced faculty, the Online MBA 2.0 Bharati Vidyapeeth builds a strong foundation in core management before allowing you to choose dual specialisations in the later semesters. With a summer internship for real-world exposure, English instruction, and flexible completion timelines, the programme prepares you to face modern business challenges with confidence.

About Bharati Vidyapeeth Deemed To Be A University, Pune

Established in 1964 by Dr. Patangrao Kadam, Bharati Vidyapeeth is a premier multidisciplinary university committed to accessible, high-quality education. Recognized by the UGC and accredited with NAAC A++, it offers diverse programs in management, engineering, law, and health sciences. Through its Centre for Distance and Online Education (CDOE), the university provides flexible, technology-driven learning for global professionals. By merging academic rigor with industry relevance, Bharati Vidyapeeth equips students with the essential skills to thrive in a dynamic global market.
